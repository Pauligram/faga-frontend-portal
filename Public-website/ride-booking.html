How FAGA rides work

📍

1. Enter your destination

Tell us where you are and where you want to go. FAGA calculates the ride fare from the selected ride type and passenger count.

🚗

2. Request a driver

FAGA sends your ride request to the operational server. Driver assignment begins when the driver service is available.

🛰️

3. Track your ride

After a driver is assigned and GPS is available, the tracking page can show the latest driver location and ride status.
